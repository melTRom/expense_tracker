# Chart of Accounts — Zero-Based Budget Line Items

25–30 line-item zero-based chart of accounts. Grouped into five tiers mapping to both zero-based and 50/30/20 thinking.

---

2. Property Tax / Escrow
3. HOA (if applicable)
4. Homeowner's Insurance
5. Home Maintenance Sinking Fund (1% of home value/year)
6. Electric
7. Gas
8. Water / Sewer / Trash
9. Internet / Cell
