# Frameworks Reference

## 1. Budgeting Frameworks

### Zero-Based Budget (Operating System)
Every baseline dollar is assigned a job before the month begins. No unallocated money remains. This is the primary operating methodology.

### 50/30/20 (Sanity Check)
- 50% Needs / 30% Wants / 20% Savings+Debt
- **Alert threshold:** Flag when needs exceed 60% of net pay — this is a structural problem signal
- Used as a diagnostic overlay, not the primary system

### Two-Tier Baseline Income Model
- **Tier 1 (Baseline):** W-2 salary (after tax) + 25th-percentile of side income from last 6–12 months
- **Tier 2 (Surge):** Anything above baseline, pre-allocated BEFORE it arrives:
  - 50% → high-interest debt
  - 20% → emergency fund
  - 15% → Roth IRA
  - 10% → 529
  - 5% → guilt-free spending

### Side Income Tax Treatment
- Set aside 25–30% of gross side income for self-employment tax (~15.3% SE tax + estimated federal income tax)
- Hold in separate tax-holding sub-account
- Texas: no state income tax (advantage)
- Quarterly estimated tax dates: **Apr 15, Jun 15, Sep 15, Jan 15**

### Encoded Principles
- **Ramsey:** Baby Steps debt urgency order (modified — cannot pause college savings for child already enrolled)
- **Sethi:** Automate aggressively, spend deliberately on what you value, cut mercilessly on what you don't; small guilt-free allowance prevents budget burnout
- **Hybrid:** Zero-based execution + 50/30/20 sanity check + Bogleheads investment order + Ramsey debt urgency + Sethi automation philosophy

## 2. FICO Credit Score Framework

### Factor Weights (Ground Truth)
| Factor | Weight | Target |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Payment History | 35% | Zero missed payments — one 30-day late drops score 60–110 pts, stays 7 years |
| Amounts Owed (Utilization) | 30% | Single-digit per card AND aggregate (800+ scorers avg ~6%) |
| Length of History | 15% | Never close oldest card; keep open with small recurring charge + autopay |
| New Credit | 10% | No new applications during credit repair window or within 12 months of planned mortgage refi |
| Credit Mix | 10% | Maintain diverse account types |

### DTI (Debt-to-Income)
- Not a FICO factor but used by every lender
- Formula: (all monthly debt payments incl. mortgage) ÷ gross monthly income
- Target: ≤36% preferred; ≤43% max for mortgage qualification
- Compute monthly

### Credit-Repair Playbook
1. Pull all 3 free reports weekly at AnnualCreditReport.com; dispute errors
2. Bring every account current and stay current
3. Drive utilization to single digits via payment timing and/or limit increase requests (soft-pull if possible)
4. Keep oldest card open with small recurring charge + autopay
5. Avoid new applications during repair window
6. Re-check FICO 30–45 days after each utilization payment

## 3. Emergency Fund Tiers

| Tier | Target | Description |
|------|--------|-------------|
| 1 — Starter EF | $2,000–$2,500 | One-month cushion + buffer for one vet emergency. Ramsey's $1,000 is too low given senior pug. |
| 2 — Working EF | 1 month essential expenses | Housing + utilities + groceries + insurance + min debt + transport. Build while attacking high-interest debt. |
| 3 — Full EF | 6 months essential expenses (~$30K) | 6 months (not 3) due to: single-income risk, 3 vehicles, senior pet, mortgage, dependent kids. |
| 4 — Sinking Funds | Dedicated sub-accounts | Pug emergency ($3K–$5K), vehicle repair ($1K–$2K), home repair fund. |

### HYSA Guidance (May 2026)
- Top HYSAs: 4.00–5.00% APY
- Reputable options: Varo (5.00%), SoFi (4.50% w/ DD), Axos (~4.21%), Amex HYSA, Marcus, Capital One 360, Ally, Bread Savings
- National average: ~0.38%
- Fed has cut rates 3x since late 2025; further cuts expected
- EF must stay liquid in HYSA
- Consider 12-month CD for funds untouched 12+ months
- All accounts: FDIC/NCUA $250K per-depositor coverage required

## 4. Bogleheads Investment Priority Order (Adapted)

1. Build starter EF + bring all debt current
2. Capture full employer 401(k) match (guaranteed 50–100% return)
3. Pay off high-interest debt (above ~7–8%, especially credit cards)
4. Max HSA if eligible (triple tax-advantaged)
5. Max Roth IRA(s) — 2026 limit $7,000/person ($8,000 if 50+); **flag Spousal Roth IRA opportunity**
6. Remainder of 401(k) to annual employee limit
7. 529 for kids
8. Pay off medium-interest debt (auto loans 4–7%)
9. Taxable brokerage for surplus
10. Optional: extra mortgage principal if rate >~5% and other priorities met

### Three-Fund Portfolio
| Asset Class | Vanguard | Fidelity | Notes |
|-------------|----------|----------|-------|
| US Total Market | VTI | FZROX | Core domestic equity |
| International | VXUS | FZILX | International diversification |
| US Total Bond | BND | FXNAX | Fixed income stability |

- Expense ratios ≤ 0.10%
- Schwab equivalents also acceptable
- **Avoid:** actively managed funds, individual stock-picking, anything with loads

### 529 Planning (Texas Specific)
- Texas has NO state-tax advantage — shop nationally
- Utah my529 and other top-rated low-fee plans commonly preferred
- SECURE 2.0: unused 529 balances up to $35K lifetime can roll to beneficiary's Roth IRA after 15 years
- For current student: cash-flow tuition first, fund 529 only with surplus after retirement priorities
- Explore: UTEP-specific scholarships, Pell Grant eligibility, Texas Tuition Promise Fund (locks tuition for younger children)

## 5. Expense-Cut Hierarchy

| Tier | Level | Examples | Impact |
|------|-------|----------|--------|
| 1 | Painless | Subscription audit ($50–$150/mo typical), cell plan renegotiation, insurance shopping, refi high-interest debt, credit card rewards optimization, generic Rx, energy efficiency | Zero lifestyle impact |
| 2 | Low-Pain | Dining-out frequency, grocery optimization (meal planning, store brands, warehouse club math), entertainment substitution, no-spend weeks | Minor lifestyle adjustment |
| 3 | Structural | Vehicle reduction, housing downsize, mortgage refinance | Major lifestyle change |
| 4 | Last Resort | Education/retirement contributions, pet quality of care | Affects long-term goals |

**Rule:** Always pair proposed cut with dollar impact AND qualitative cost, presented as trade-off. Cut mercilessly on what you don't value; spend deliberately on what you do.

## 6. Vehicle TCO Analysis Framework

### Per-Vehicle Monthly TCO
Formula: loan payment + insurance + fuel + maintenance + (estimated annual repairs ÷ 12) + depreciation

### Decision Logic
- Three vehicles for one driver = structurally expensive
- **2022 Sentra SR:** If auto rate < ~5%, pay minimums (mathematically rational). If > ~6–7%, accelerate payoff after match/Roth.
- **Van & Chrysler:** Annual keep-vs-sell calc: (annual repair + insurance + registration) vs (sale value + insurance savings + reduced maintenance). **Sell trigger:** single repair > 50% of vehicle market value.
- **Insurance:** Shop full quotes every 12 months from 3+ carriers. Raise deductibles to $1,000 if EF supports. Drop comp/collision on vehicles worth < ~$4,000. Bundle with home.
- **Depreciation:** Pull KBB/Edmunds values quarterly for net-worth accuracy.

## 7. Pet Financial Planning

### Senior Pug (9 years)
- **Breed risks:** BOAS/airway, eye injuries, skin-fold infections, allergies, spinal issues
- **Insurance:** Marginal for 9-year-old pug (premiums $80–$150+/mo, pre-existing exclusions). Quote from MetLife Pet, Pumpkin, AKC, Spot, Lemonade. Only if < ~$60/mo with reasonable terms.
- **Recommended:** Dedicated sinking fund $3,000–$5,000 in HYSA + accident-only insurance if cheap
- **Annual routine budget:** ~$1,200–$2,000 (food, prevention, 2 vet visits, dental, meds)
- **Prompt:** Establish humane endpoint budget conversation proactively

### Kitten (3 months)
- **First-year all-in:** $1,000–$2,000
  - Vaccine series: $150–$350
  - Spay/neuter: $75–$500 (TX low-cost clinics like TCAP: $100–$200)
  - Microchip: $15–$60
  - FeLV/FIV test: ~$30–$50
  - Prevention: ~$200/yr
  - Supplies: $200–$400
  - Wellness visits: 1–2
- **Insurance:** $12–$32/month — enroll BEFORE any condition documented in medical record
